# Invoingo — Good work. Great finish.

## The nine planning answers
1. Invoingo is a local invoicing workspace for independent businesses: estimates, products and services, invoices, sharing, payment records, expenses and reports.
2. Strongest product line: “Your whole workday. In hand.” The impressive moment is a saved service becoming an invoice and a clear payment record.
3. Visual hook: forest-green lettering on a pale canvas; an oversized lime check transforms into the arrow from Invoingo's identity.
4. Show invoice line items, invoice preview, sending and tracking, paid state and reports. Recreate the actual application's labels and hierarchy at film-readable scale.
5. Exactly 20 seconds, landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps.
6. Cinematic / polished: a confident product-design showreel with carefully choreographed floating UI, fast camera moves and settled typography.
7. Original 120 BPM electronic score: warm seventh chords, rounded bass, crisp drums, glassy plucks, whooshes and payment chime. No narration.
8. Share caption: “Good work. Great finish. Meet Invoingo: estimates, invoices, payment tracking and your business in hand. Explore the web preview; iOS and Android apps are in development.”
9. Flow: select saved items → build and send an invoice → see payment recorded and business reports.

## Reference study
Source: https://x.com/Muse/status/2097399178376671666/video/1
The downloaded reference contains 1,983 decoded frames, about 82.6 seconds at 24 fps. All frames were inspected in chronological contact sheets, with larger one-second overview frames. Reference is study material only; no reference footage, logos, mascot, UI or audio is used in the delivered film.
- Frames 0–58: app icon opens into a field of small objects, then contracts into type.
- 59–245: centered text builds in short phrases; small icons occupy positions within words; staggered exits preserve reading time.
- 246–418: floating chat pills grow naturally and the camera moves into the working UI.
- 419–719: isolated UI cards tilt, enlarge, stack, then resolve into a clean “Done” confirmation.
- 720–1247: short capability typography alternates with close-ups of a working product; camera follows the specific action.
- 1248–1677: chat-to-calendar transition, zoom into a selected tile, confirmation payoff.
- 1678–1982: changing capability words, orbital icon cascade, return to clean centered identity, final logo lockup.
Adaptation: use Invoingo's document → send → payment objects, shared visual anchors, restrained depth, macro UI views, phrase-by-phrase type and a satisfying confirmation. Use the project's green identity instead of Muse's blue, and compress the product story into twenty seconds.

## Visual identity
Forest #163300; lime #9fe870; mist #e2f6d5; warm paper #f5f6f2; white #ffffff. Inter 900 for display, Inter 400/600/700 for UI. Soft directional shadows, substantial round corners and pills. No stock footage or unrelated imagery.

## Storyboard
1. 0–2.5s / GOOD WORK. Receipt fragments and invoice primitives fan around a centered kinetic headline. Invoingo brand above it. Headline settles by 0.55s. Percussive opening and ascending plucks.
2. 2.5–7s / MAKE IT. YOURS. Camera lands on a large invoice editor. Saved services arrive in sequence: Brand identity $1,200; Social templates $300. The $1,500 total holds. Pointer presses Preview. Natural text timings; two rows land at 3.5s and 4.0s and remain readable through 6.6s.
3. 7–10.5s / SEND IT. A polished invoice rotates into the send surface; a lime send pulse travels through a curved path; tracking rows arrive Sent, Delivered, Opened. Simulated demo states. The full set holds from 8.8–10.2s.
4. 10.5–14s / GREAT FINISH. A circular check draws itself; payment card rises with $1,500.00 and Paid in full. Supporting text: Payment recorded. “Illustrative payment” remains visible. Resolution chord and original three-note chime.
5. 14–17s / YOUR BUSINESS. IN HAND. Reports, expenses and saved-items cards arrange in depth. Chart bars grow sequentially; income figure and labels settle by 15.0s. Fictional values only.
6. 17–20s / INVOINGO. Product fragments retreat; a lime brand tile turns into place; oversized wordmark and “Good work. Great finish.” hold through the end. CTA: Explore the web preview. iOS + Android in development. Music resolves and fades to silence.

## Audio direction
Original score generated locally, avoiding reliance on bundled music with undocumented distribution terms. Stereo 48 kHz master. 120 BPM gives a 0.5-second beat grid. Strong transition cues at 2.5, 7, 10.5, 14 and 17 seconds; UI row clicks at 3.5 and 4.0, tracking accents at 8, 8.5 and 9.0. A computed RMS envelope subtly modulates the existing stage halo, never the readable text. SFX are synthesized as part of the score and match implemented motion. No voiceover, sampled songs or reference audio.
