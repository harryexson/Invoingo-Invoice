# Visual reference guide — production v2

This guide records the supplied visual authorities for the new office presentation. Use these assets for every new generated shot and still keyframe.

## Supplied references

- `reference-inputs/cheryl-portrait.png` — close facial reference. Cheryl is a Black woman with a warm medium-brown complexion, short natural black curls, and gold hoop earrings. Use this for facial identity, skin, hair, and expression.
- `reference-inputs/cheryl-angle-triptych.png` — one three-panel character sheet showing the same Cheryl front-on, in three-quarter view, and in profile. It provides the additional face angles, full-body proportions, and wardrobe reference: a dark forest-green pantsuit, ivory crew-neck top, and nude heels. The panels are angle references, not three people.
- `reference-inputs/modern-home-office.png` — environment reference. Preserve the warm wood desk, open dark shelving, books, plants, framed artwork, large right-side window, soft daylight, and welcoming lived-in atmosphere. Its bytes match the previously uploaded office asset `729f92bb-747a-4e60-99fd-31892b167421`, so reuse that Higgsfield upload.

The two character files provide four useful views in total: one close portrait plus the three angles in the triptych. The office file is the separate environment reference.

## Continuity rules

Keep Cheryl's facial identity, short natural curls, complexion, approximate age, body proportions, green suit, ivory top, and hoop earrings consistent. Match the office's warm wood, plants, open shelves, artwork, window direction, and daylight. Extend only what a shot needs, using the reference layout and palette. Keep the phone, desk, chair, and Cheryl's position physically consistent between adjacent shots.

The earlier seated keyframe and previous production prompts describe a different outfit and office. They are superseded for visual identity and setting by the supplied references. Do not use that seated image as the first frame for the new take. The older upload IDs `3e759f24-c057-4a94-8616-95efc437d3fc` and `b0dad728-eddb-4759-83f4-05c63ada4a02` belong to those superseded character references; do not attach them to new renders.
