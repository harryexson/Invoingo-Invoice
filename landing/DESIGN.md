# Invoingo launch page

Direction: an independent-business campaign inspired by the supplied Wise reference: https://styles.refero.design/style/367c0c6e-73a7-441c-a8ff-91d139ac60dc

Forest #163300, lime #9fe870, spruce #054d28, mist #e2f6d5, fog #e8ebe6, paper #ffffff. Inter Black provides a licensed substitute for the reference's proprietary display type; body text uses Inter. Large, tight headlines and flat, alternating color fields give the page its rhythm. Pill actions and generous whitespace carry through the product UI.

The signature interaction is a floating invoice phone whose state progresses from estimate to invoice to payment. A custom 20-second silent motion film tells the same story. The industry selector uses the application's real 45-industry catalog. All app calls to action open the working web preview; mobile platform buttons explain the iOS/Android release status without implying App Store availability.

AI asset: assets/maker-studio.png, created with the built-in image generation tool. Prompt: premium documentary-style wide photograph of a female furniture maker with medium-brown skin and curly tied-back hair, in a forest-green work shirt and tan apron, checking a smartphone in a sunlit carpentry studio; completed oak chair, tools, pale lime wall, tactile natural textures, person center-right and negative space left; 16:9; no text, brands, or watermarks.

The video is an original locally rendered motion-graphics film, not a Veo/Kling generation. No authenticated external video generator was available. Its source is tools/render_campaign.py; the AI image appears in the final scene. Rendered output is assets/invoingo-film.mp4.

Accessibility: semantic navigation, keyboard controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, global pause control, labeled demo illustrations, captions/transcript for the silent film, and responsive phone-scale layouts.

Campaign update: forest-and-lime animated hero, morphing orbit, payment ripple, staggered chart bars, live three-phone journey preview with iOS/Android switches, eight feature atlas links, keyboard-accessible journey tabs, new AI editorial painter image (assets/workday-editorial.png), and an original five-scene Inter motion film. Motion pause and reduced-motion settings apply throughout. Expo device preview instructions are at /expo.html.
