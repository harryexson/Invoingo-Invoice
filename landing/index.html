LET’S CLEAR THINGS UP

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Can I use Invoingo right now?+

Yes. Open the web preview to explore the working application. The native iOS and Android apps are in development; App Store and Google Play downloads are not available yet.

Does it fit my kind of business?+

Choose from 45 industries, including trades, creative services, retail, hospitality and professional services. Start with suggested billable items, add your own products or services, and set your own prices.

Can clients pay with their phone?+

You can add a Stripe-hosted invoice URL to an invoice. Eligible clients can use the payment methods offered by Stripe, including supported mobile wallets. The new test integration verifies Stripe events on the server. Refresh provider status in the workspace to import verified payments. Live payments remain disabled.

Can I turn an estimate into an invoice?+

Yes. Create an estimate with your services and products, then convert it into an invoice. You can adjust quantities, rates, taxes and discounts to match the work.

Where is my business data stored?+

This web preview stores data in your browser on this device. It does not sync between devices. Use the app’s backup and export tools to keep a copy before clearing browser data or moving to another device.
