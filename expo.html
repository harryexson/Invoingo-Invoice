Open the mobile preview in Expo Go on iOS or Android.

  1. Install or update Expo Go on your phone.
  2. Connect your phone and this computer to the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Scan the QR using the iPhone camera, or the Expo Go scanner on Android.
  4. Tap Explore the mobile app in the preview.
Keep both the phone-preview server and Expo Metro running. The Expo shell displays the responsive app in a native WebView with fictional demo data. Native device testing and App Store / Play Store releases remain separate steps. Browser storage inside Expo Go is separate from your desktop workspace.Open the mobile browser preview ↗
